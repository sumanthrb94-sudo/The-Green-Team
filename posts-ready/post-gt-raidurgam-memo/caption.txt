A 6.29-acre multi-use parcel at TGIIC's Raidurg e-auction has sold for ₹1,490.73 crore — a new all-time high for Hyderabad land.

The successful bidder, Gowra Ventures Pvt Ltd, plans to develop IT office towers and mixed-use infrastructure on the site. Their final bid of ₹237 crore per acre ran 70.5% above the ₹139 crore reserve price, comfortably surpassing the previous record of ₹177 crore per acre set at an earlier 2025 Raidurg auction.

To put the number in context: ₹237 cr / acre is roughly 3–4× HITEC City, 5× Banjara Hills, and around 50× the indicative rate for forest-adjacent land on Hyderabad's outer ring.

Why this 4-km strip is rewriting Telangana's price discovery:

— Knowledge City, anchored by HCU, research campuses, AMD India HQ, and Microsoft.
— Metro Phase 2 corridor inbound.
— Scarcity. TGIIC's parcels are the last institutional-grade land in West Hyderabad.
