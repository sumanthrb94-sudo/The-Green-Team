# Website Content Pack

## Audit summary

The site already has a strong visual direction, a clear portfolio, and credible proof-led positioning. The biggest content opportunity is to make the first screen explain the offer faster: **what is being curated, where it is, why it is different, and what the visitor should do next**. The existing copy is elegant but occasionally reads like internal positioning rather than a direct buyer promise.

The revised copy keeps the premium, editorial tone while improving clarity, search relevance, and conversion intent. It avoids unsupported guarantees and keeps every numerical claim tied to the site's existing data model.

## Homepage hero

**Eyebrow:** Independent curators · Hyderabad

**Headline:** The forest is the address.

**Supporting copy:** Forest-adjacent plots, homes, and retreats near Hyderabad — selected for cleaner air, quieter surroundings, real access, and paperwork you can verify. We do the first round of checking. You decide where to go.

**Primary CTA:** Request an adviser call

**Secondary CTA:** Explore the sanctuaries

## Portfolio section

**Eyebrow:** Curated portfolio

**Heading:** Three sanctuaries. Chosen for how they feel — and what they can prove.

**Supporting copy:** Every listing is visited, checked, and described in plain language. Compare the setting, access, air, noise, title, and development stage before you spend a weekend on a site visit.

**CTA:** Explore all sanctuaries

## Proof section

**Heading:** A greener address needs more than a green brochure.

**Supporting copy:** We look past the render. The Green Team checks the surroundings, the approach road, the paperwork, the developer's track record, and the claims behind every number before a project enters the portfolio.

## Adviser section

**Heading:** Tell us what you are looking for.

**Supporting copy:** Share your budget, preferred corridor, and what you want to build or live in. An adviser will send the shortest useful shortlist — not a catalogue of every project in Hyderabad.

## SEO metadata

**Title:** Forest-Adjacent Homes and Land Near Hyderabad | The Green Team

**Description:** Discover verified forest-adjacent plots, homes, and retreats near Hyderabad. Compare air, access, noise, paperwork, and development stage across curated sanctuaries including Agartha, SYL Residences, and Dates County.

## Content principles

Use specific locations and property types naturally, lead with buyer outcomes, separate verified facts from future plans, and avoid language that implies assured returns, guaranteed appreciation, or environmental certification unless the underlying source explicitly supports it.
