## Caption notes

- Hero word #3 ("curate") is the brand-positioning payoff. Gold weight
  on it should be one notch heavier than #1 and #2.
- The crown phrases on beats 1 and 2 are diagnostic ("most people do
  X"). The crown phrase on beat 3 is brand ("WE SHOW YOU THREE").
  The visual rhythm of that "WE" landing big behind the head is the
  emotional anchor — viewers feel the shift from market to curator.
