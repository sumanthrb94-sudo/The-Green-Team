# UGC cast — Tara

The recurring presenter for The Green Team's short-form ads. Chosen from a
twelve-face casting round; she is **H · Tara, 26**.

- `tara-reference.png` — **the load-bearing file.** Every future frame, in
  every format, is generated with this image as the identity reference. It is
  what keeps her the same person across a year of ads, so it is checked in at
  full resolution and never re-encoded or cropped. If it is lost, the
  character is lost with it — a re-roll produces a different woman.
- `tara-agartha-seed.png` — her composited into `public/gallery/agartha/3.webp`
  as a selfie-framed still, used as the first frame for the Veo 3.1 clip. The
  pattern to copy for other projects: identity reference + a project render,
  never a prompt describing the property from memory.
- `alt-*.jpg` — the five runners-up, kept only so a second presenter can be
  cast later without re-running the round. Contact-sheet resolution; not
  usable as identity references.

## Rules this cast works under

She is **not a customer**. She is a host, and the copy has to stay inside
that: she can describe what we check and what a place is like, but she cannot
report an experience of buying, owning, or earning from anything here.

**Disclose her.** ASCI's influencer guidelines treat virtual and AI-generated
presenters as requiring disclosure, and "UGC" is precisely the framing that
makes it sharp — the format's persuasive power *is* the implication that a
real person filmed it. Every placement carries a visible "Virtual host ·
AI-generated" label.

No price, rate, appreciation figure or RERA number comes out of her mouth in
a clip this short; they need context an eight-second cut cannot give, and the
assured-return framing is one thegreenteam.in refuses in writing. Claims she
does make — AQI 12, 45 minutes to the city, the air/quiet/access/title check —
are ones the site already publishes and stands behind.

Sources are architectural **renders**, not photographs of finished homes, so
nothing built from them may imply completed construction.

Nothing here is served by the app; `marketing/` sits outside `public/` and is
not part of the deploy.
